LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Franchise agreement

Signed before fit-out begins. Drafted to protect the brand and give the franchisee a clear, exclusive operating window.

Term

5 years from the date of commercial opening, with a renewal option subject to audit scores and mutual consent.

Territory

A mapped exclusive zone (typically a city hub or defined catchment). No second UBI dine-in in that map during the term.

Brand licence

Right to use the UBI name, logo, packaging, and menu solely at the approved outlet. Sub-franchising is not allowed without written consent.

Performance

Minimum opening hours, hygiene scores, and recipe compliance. Persistent SOP breaches can trigger cure notices or termination.

Renewal

Low-cost extension after a performance review. Fit-out refresh may be required to match the current brand lookbook.

Exit

Transfer of the outlet to a new franchisee is possible with franchisor approval, training of the incoming partner, and a transfer fee.

RULES THE FRANCHISEE MUST FOLLOW

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

These are non-negotiable brand rules. They protect taste, hygiene, and the guest experience at every UBI table.

01

Centralized spice formulations

Core biryani masalas, gravies, and saffron seasoning must come only from the franchisor commissary. No local substitutions.

02

Slow dum handi discipline

Every batch is dum-pukht in sealed heavy-bottom brass handis. Reheating leftover biryani in a microwave is forbidden.

03

Hygiene & quality audits

Unannounced mystery visits and quarterly hygiene scores. Failed audits require a written corrective plan within 7 days.

04

Guest experience & POS

Brand uniforms, brass tableware, approved takeaway packs, and the central cloud billing software only.

05

Approved menu & pricing band

Core menu items cannot be dropped. Local specials need written approval. Prices stay inside the national band.

06

Staff & hours

Kitchen leads complete UBI certification. Outlets keep posted dining hours (typically 11:30 AM–11:30 PM) unless HQ agrees otherwise.
