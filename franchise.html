QUALITY INTEGRITY

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

Rules, culinary discipline, and brand standards every franchisee must uphold to protect our royal reputation.

01

Centralized Spice Formulations

All core biryani spice masalas, gravies, and saffron seasoning must be procured exclusively through the franchisor's central commissary to ensure 100% taste consistency nationwide.

02

Slow Dum Handi Cooking Discipline

Every batch must adhere to traditional dum-pukht protocols in sealed heavy-bottom brass handis. No reheating or microwave shortcuts are ever permitted in our kitchens.

03

Quarterly Hygiene & Quality Audits

Our quality assurance team conducts unannounced mystery inspections and hygiene assessments every quarter to guarantee uncompromised 5-star food safety.

04

Unified Guest Experience & POS

Mandatory use of standard brand uniforms, brass tableware presentation, centralized cloud billing software, and verified packaging materials for takeaways.
