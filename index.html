OUR STORY & PHILOSOPHY

One Nation, One Emotion, One Plate

In a country of countless cultures and vibrant traditions, one emotion remains timeless: the profound love for biryani. We founded United Biryanis of India on a single belief — a great biryani is a celebration that unites families, friends, and communities from every corner of India.

We celebrate authentic vintage cooking methods — slow-steamed in sealed brass handis over simmering embers with pure desi ghee, royal saffron, and hand-ground spices. Whether it is the bold fiery depth of Hyderabadi Dum, the subtle aromatic finesse of Lucknow, or the coastal whispers of Thalassery, every creation brings India’s legendary biryani heritage together under one roof.
